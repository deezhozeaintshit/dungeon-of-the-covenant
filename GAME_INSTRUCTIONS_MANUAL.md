# 📖 DUNGEON OF THE COVENANT — COMPLETE ALL-FEATURES INSTRUCTIONS MANUAL

Welcome to the official Player & Operator Manual for **Dungeon of the Covenant**. This guide covers every control, class ability, elemental combo reaction, dungeon zone, boss mechanic, forge upgrade, and progression system in the game.

## 🚀 1. Getting Started & Joining a Multiplayer Raid

1. **Launch the Game**:
   - **PC Browser**: Open `http://localhost:3000`
   - **Mobile / Remote Devices**: Open `https://kernel-connectors-computer-travelling.trycloudflare.com`
   - **Official Promo Video Trailer**: Open `http://localhost:3000/promo.html` (or click **`🎬 WATCH OFFICIAL AAA PROMO TRAILER`** on the Lobby screen).
2. **Choose Your Hero Name & Covenant Class**:
   - Enter your hero name (up to 14 characters) and tap one of the **6 Covenant Classes** (*Juggernaut*, *Radiant Cleric*, *Shadowblade*, *Pyromancer*, *Deadeye*, or *Dreadweaver*).
3. **Create or Join a Chamber (2–8 Players)**:
   - **To Host**: Click **`CREATE NEW CHAMBER`**. A large **4-Letter Chamber Code** (e.g., `K9X2`) will appear on screen.
   - **To Join from Phones/Other PCs**: Enter the 4-Letter Chamber Code into the **`4-LETTER CODE`** box and tap **`JOIN CHAMBER`**.
   - **AI Companion Fill**: When the host clicks **`DESCEND INTO THE CRYPT`**, any empty slots (up to a 4-hero party) are automatically filled by intelligent **AI Companion Bots** wearing Mythic 3D Auras who follow your path, react to your pings, dodge telegraphs, and revive you when downed!

## 🎮 2. Complete Controls Guide (Mobile & Desktop PC)

### 📱 Mobile / Touchscreen Controls
- **Left Floating Virtual Joystick**: Drag anywhere on the left half of the screen to move your hero smoothly in any direction (automatically aligned to your current camera angle).
- **Right Thumb Ability Arc**:
  - **⚔️ Basic Attack (Large Center Button)**: Fires your class primary melee/ranged strike toward your locked target.
  - **💥 Skill 1 / 🛡️ Skill 2 / ⚡ Skill 3 (Ultimate)**: Triggers your 3 signature class abilities (with live cooldown timer overlays).
  - **💨 DASH Button (`#btn-dash`)**: Executes a **Tactical Dodge-Roll (`4.8m` burst)** with **`0.45s` of Invulnerability (`i-frames`)**.
  - **📍 Party Ping Wheel**: Opens the 4-way Tactical Ping Radial (`⚔️ Attack`, `🛡️ Regroup`, `💎 Loot`, `🆘 Help`).
- **Touch Camera Controls**:
  - **2-Finger Pinch**: Zoom camera in/out (`12m` close-up to `44m` tactical overview).
  - **Top-Display Camera Toolbar**: Tap `🔍−`, `🔍+`, `↺`, `↻`, or `🗺️ FAR` beneath the Mini-Map.
  - **Tap Any Enemy in 3D**: Locks your red **Target Reticle** onto that specific monster or boss.

### 💻 Desktop PC (Keyboard & Mouse) Controls
- **`W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or Arrow Keys**: Move Hero (Camera-Relative).
- **`Spacebar` or Left-Click on Enemy**: Basic Attack & Target Lock.
- **`1`, `2`, `3` Keys**: Cast **Skill 1**, **Skill 2**, and **Skill 3 (Ultimate)**.
- **`Shift` Key**: **Tactical Dash / Dodge-Roll** (`3.0s` cooldown, grants `0.45s` Invulnerability `i-frames`).
- **`Q` / `E` Keys (or Right-Click + Drag)**: Rotate Camera Orbit Left / Right around your Hero.
- **`Mouse Scroll Wheel` (or `+` / `-` Keys)**: Smoothly Zoom Camera In / Out.

## 🗺️ 4. The 7-Zone Citadel, Twin Soul-Seals & Environmental Traps

Your expedition begins in the **Covenant Atrium (`z = +22`)** and progresses southward toward the **Soul-Forge Sanctum (`z = -75`)**, tracked live on your **Top-Right Mini-Map** and **Top-Center Quest Banner (`#hud-quest-banner`)**:

1. **Zone 1 & 2 — Covenant Atrium & Pillared Narthex**:
   - Battle the initial Skeletal Vanguard and Bone Archers (who kite and shoot green necrotic arrows).
2. **Zone 3 — The Grand Crossroads Hub (`0, -14`)**:
   - Central four-way hub featuring a Restoration Elixir cache. Branches West, East, and South.
3. **Zone 4 — West Wing: The Blood Reliquary (`x = -30` to `-54`)**:
   - Watch out for the **Reliquary Blood-Spike Trap (`-24, -14`)** in the corridor!
   - Defeat **Mini-Boss: Vorgath, the Bone-Executioner** (casts `3.8m` Crimson Guillotine telegraphs).
   - **Rewards**: Shatters **1 of Malakor's 2 Sanctum Soul-Seals**, activates the **Shrine of Blood Fury (`+25% Party DMG`)**, and drops **🗡️ Vorgath's Crimson Greataxe Relic (`+35% DMG, +15% Lifesteal`)**.
4. **Zone 5 — East Wing: The Alchemist's Vault (`x = +30` to `+54`)**:
   - Watch out for the **Vault Arcane Pylon Trap (`+24, -14`)** in the corridor!
   - Defeat **Mini-Boss: Arch-Lich Malthor** (fires 3-prong cyan soul-bolt volleys).
   - **Rewards**: Shatters **Malakor's 2nd Sanctum Soul-Seal**, activates the **Shrine of Astral Aegis (`+30% Max HP & War Horn Recharge`)**, and drops **🔮 Malthor's Astral Phylactery Relic (`+30% Cooldown Haste, +250 HP`)**.
5. **Zone 6 — The Abyssal Bridge & Antechamber of Chains (`z = -26` to `-56`)**:
   - Cross the narrow stone bridge spanning a boiling lava chasm while dodging **4 Timed Abyssal Flame Geysers** (`⚡ TRAP DODGED!` when dashing with `i-frames`, or lure Cinder Thralls into the geysers for `65` damage!).
6. **Zone 7 — The Soul-Forge Sanctum (`0, -75`)**:
   - Confront **Malakor, Soul-Forge Warden**.
   - **Twin Soul-Seal Mechanic**: With 2 Seals intact, Malakor has a `50%` Damage Ward. With 1 Seal broken, his ward drops to `25%`. With **both Seals shattered (`0/2`)**, his ward breaks completely and he suffers **`+20% Bonus Vulnerability`**!

## ⚒️ 5. Progression, Mid-Run Forge, War Horn & Covenant Emporium

### ⚒️ Mid-Run Covenant Soul-Forge (`⚒️ FORGE` Button in Top HUD)
Spend Gold (`💰`) looted from enemies and treasure chests at any time during a run:
- **⚔️ Temper Weapon (`80g`)**: Permanently increases your hero's Damage by **`+25%`** per rank.
- **🛡️ Temper Armor (`80g`)**: Permanently increases your Max HP by **`+180`** and heals you immediately.
- **🧪 Covenant Surge (`60g`)**: Heals the entire party for **`65% Max HP`**, revives any downed allies, and resets all ability cooldowns to `0s`.

### 🔥 Adrenaline Kill-Streak & Soul Overdrive
- Every attack has an **`18%` Base Critical Strike Chance (`1.75x` Damage)**.
- Slaying **4+ enemies within `8.5s`** ignites **🔥 SOUL OVERDRIVE**, granting **`+22% Movement Speed`**, **`+25% Crit Chance (`43%` Total)`**, and a blazing 3D floor ring!

### 📯 War Horn, Last Stand & Clutch Revives
- **📯 War Horn**: Tap the **`📯 WAR HORN`** button in the top bar to freeze time for all monsters and bosses (`timeScale = 0.05`) for **10 seconds** while your party moves and attacks at full speed!
- **Clutch Revive**: Stand within `3.2m` of a downed teammate for `3.5s` to resurrect them.
- **Last Stand**: If all teammates fall except one hero, **LAST STAND** activates automatically, granting the sole survivor **`+45% Movement Speed`** to kite the boss and pull off a miraculous rescue.

### 💎 Covenant Emporium, 3D Mythic Auras & Persistent Soul-Tree (`💎 SHOP` Button)
- **Earnable & Purchasable Soul Shards (`💎`)**: Start with `300 💎` and earn `+5 💎` per mob, `+35 💎` per Wing Mini-Boss, and `+120 💎` per Boss kill.
- **3D Mythic Hero Auras**: Unlock and equip **Infernal Demon-Crown (`200 💎`)**, **Astral Void-Star (`250 💎`)**, **Plague-Wraith Shroud (`300 💎`)**, or **Sovereign Seraph Wings (`450 💎`)** to render real-time 3D wings, crowns, and orbiting crystals on your character in multiplayer.
- **Permanent Account Soul-Tree**: Spend `100 💎` per rank on **Soul of Might (`+12% Base DMG`)**, **Soul of Vitality (`+150 Base HP`)**, or **Soul of Chronos (`+10% Cooldown Haste`)**—saved automatically to your profile across all future sessions!
