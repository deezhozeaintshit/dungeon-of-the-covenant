## Technical Specifications

### Model Requirements
- **Polygon Count:** 
  - Minion: 5,000-15,000
  - Knight/Serpent/Guard: 20,000-50,000
  - Boss: 80,000-150,000
- **Texture Resolution:** 2K-4K
- **Format:** GLB/GLTF (preferred) or FBX
- **Rig:** Humanoid rig with custom bones for non-humanoids
- **LODs:** 3 levels minimum

### Animation Standards
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps base, 60fps for high-action
- **Blend Times:** 0.1-0.3 seconds
- **Root Motion:** Enable for movement
- **IK:** Foot IK for terrain adaptation
