WHAT WAS BUILT
-------------
New modules (client/js/):
  biomes.js      - 4 themed templates (crypt / cavern / forge / throne_room):
                   prop placement rules, material palette keys, lighting mood,
                   FogExp2 color+density, particle language, torch language.
                   Exports: BIOME_TEMPLATES, BIOME_IDS, LEGACY_BIOME_MAP,
                   resolveBiomeTemplate(id), ZONE_DEFS (8 citadel zones),
                   KEEP_OUTS, assignZoneBiomes(seed, themeId?), dominantTemplate().
  propPlacer.js  - PropPlacer class. Procedural PBR prop builders with shared
                   geometry/material caches (sarcophagus, bone_pile,
                   crypt_pillar, altar, stalagmite, stalactite_cluster,
                   rock_spire, ember_vent, anvil, lava_channel, hanging_chain,
                   crucible, throne, grand_pillar, banner, brazier, chest,
                   torch, rubble) + seeded rejection sampling against KEEP_OUTS
                   + per-zone floor overlay textures.
  atmosphere.js  - Atmosphere class. Owns scene.fog (FogExp2 per dominant
                   biome), ambient+hemisphere mood lights, per-zone
                   THREE.Points particle systems (dust/ash/embers/motes),
                   flame sprites + flicker (capped at 8 real PointLights),
                   emissive pulse for lava/ember vents.
