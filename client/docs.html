The Basics

◆

Dungeon of the Covenant is a real-time multiplayer action RPG played in your browser. No install, no launcher: open the link, pick a hero, and descend. Parties of 2 to 8 players fight through procedurally generated dungeon floors, complete objectives, and face multi-phase biome bosses.

  • Co-op first. Everything is built for parties. Empty slots are filled by AI companion bots so you can start immediately, and real players who join mid-match via Quick Play take over bot slots.
  • Server-authoritative. Health, damage, loot, XP, and rewards are all computed on the server. There is no client-side cheating, and disconnects cannot corrupt your account.
  • Every run is different. Dungeon layouts, prop placement, objectives, and enemy packs are seeded per run. The dungeon also learns (see Living Dungeon).

Hero Classes

Six heroes available from the start. Three more are sealed in the Covenant Vault (see Meta-Progression).

◆

🛡️ Juggernaut

Tank

Armored titan that controls crowds and shatters frozen targets with devastating hammer impacts.

  • Shield Slam: stuns enemies ahead, shatters frozen foes for 250% damage
  • Iron Bastion: taunts nearby enemies, shields allies with 40% damage resistance
  • Seismic Vortex: drags the whole room into a tight cluster for ally AoE

✨ Radiant Cleric

Healer

Holy champion that purges the darkness and empowers teammate strikes with radiant explosions.

  • Luminary Lance: piercing light beam that damages enemies and heals allies in a line
  • Sanctuary Ward: golden glyph that rapidly regenerates ally health
  • Judgement Brand: branded targets erupt in radiant bursts whenever teammates hit them

🗡️ Shadowblade

Rogue

Lethal assassin that coats fields in flammable pitch and tears through vulnerable foes.

  • Shadowstep: teleports behind the target with a vicious bleeding strike
  • Tar Bomb: flammable tar slick (60% slow), ignites into an inferno with fire
  • Eviscerate: 300% bonus damage against burning or crowd-controlled targets

🔥 Pyromancer

Mage

Master of elemental reactions. Freezes enemies solid and detonates tar with explosive hellfire.

  • Frost Nova: freezes all nearby enemies solid for 3.5 seconds
  • Fireball: explosive projectile, ignites Tar Bombs into an Infernal Conflagration
  • Chaos Meteor: massive delayed orbital bombardment with extreme knockback

🏹 Deadeye

Ranger

Sharpshooter that unleashes arrow storms and conceals allies for clutch rescues.

  • Rapid Volley: fan of 7 piercing arrows, rapid radiant procs on branded targets
  • Pinning Trap: roots enemies in place and shreds their armor
  • Smoke Veil: smoke cloud granting stealth and 100% evasion for safe revives

💀 Dreadweaver

Necromancer

Dark sorcerer that harvests life essence and detonates fallen corpses into toxic clouds.

  • Bone Spikes: linear barrage of piercing bone spears that cause bleeding
  • Soul Drain: channels health from the target, redistributing it to all allies
  • Corpse Explosion: detonates fallen enemy corpses into massive toxic bursts

Biomes

Every descent draws from four themed dungeon languages, each with its own props, lighting, hazards, and boss.

◆

🦴 The Sunken Crypt

Bone vaults and cold blue mist. Skeletal enemies rise from the ossuaries: brittle but relentless, dealing cold damage.

❄️ The Ember Cavern

Rough rock and ember-orange underlight. Crystalline, shard-infused enemies strike fast across the ice with cold damage.

🔥 The Soul-Forge

Anvils, lava channels, and hanging chains. Ember-infused enemies hit 15% harder with fire damage. Home of the Cinder Thrall elite.

👑 The Covenant Throne Room

Grand pillars, war banners, and braziers. The seat of the Covenant Sovereign, where the deepest bargains are struck.

Deeper floors scale multiplicatively: by floor 5, packs hit roughly 1.9x health and 1.6x damage versus floor 1. Plan your oaths and gear accordingly.

Enemies & Bosses

◆

Enemy packs

Melee bruisers, ranged harassers, and swarming packs, each reskinned and retuned per biome (Skeletal, Crystalline, Ember-Infused variants). Enemies run a real AI state machine: they patrol, chase, attack, and flee when outmatched.

Elites

Elites always carry at least one affix (two from floor 4 on), shown as a colored aura:

  • Swift — much faster movement and attacks
  • Brutal — massively increased damage
  • Vampiric — heals from the damage it deals
  • Shielded — protected by an absorb shield
  • Explosive — detonates on death, clear the blast zone

Named threats

  • Vorgath, Bone-Executioner — West Wing warden, an elite executioner
  • Arch-Lich Malthor — East Wing warden, an elite lich
  • Cinder Thrall — Soul-Forge elite, a molten obsidian colossus (1100 HP) whose strikes set you burning

Combat, XP & Abilities

◆

Combat is real-time and positional. Basic attacks chain into abilities, abilities chain into each other: frozen enemies shatter for bonus damage, tar ignites, corpses detonate, branded targets erupt. Experiment with cross-class combos.

  • XP and leveling: kills and objectives grant XP. Level up mid-run to grow stronger immediately.
  • Ability upgrades: leveling offers ability choices and blessings that improve your three class abilities across the run.
  • Downed, not dead: when you fall you enter a downed state with a revive timer. Allies can revive you (Deadeye's Smoke Veil makes this safe). If the timer expires, you respawn at the soul-seal.
  • Damage numbers, kill feed, buffs: the HUD tracks your vitals, active buffs, recent kills, and floating damage numbers so you can read every fight.

Loot & Gear

Server-authoritative drops. What the server rolls is what you get, no exceptions.

◆

Enemies, elites, and bosses drop gear across three slots: weapon, armor, and relic. Press F near a drop to pick it up, I to open your 40-slot inventory, compare tooltips included.

Higher rarities roll stat affixes: bonus damage, max HP, critical chance, lifesteal, cooldown haste, armor, and elemental resistance. Elites and bosses drop from biome-weighted tables, so hunting specific biomes targets specific loot.

Covenant Oaths

The signature system. Kneel at a shrine mid-run and swear a binding oath to a dark power: real power, real price, and a behavioral term the dungeon enforces.

◆

Oath shrines (the Obelisk of the First Bargain and the Altar of the Second Price) appear in the dungeon. Each oath grants a run-long benefit, inflicts a run-long cost, and binds you to a behavioral term. Break the term and the oath curses you.

🩸 Oath of the Crimson Tithe

Benefit: +35% damage dealt, permanent this run.
Cost: max HP permanently reduced by 20% this run (never restored).
Term: the Tithe must be fed. Deal damage at least once every 90 seconds while foes remain, or it collects from you.

🕯️ Oath of the Hollow Saint

Benefit: healing aura. You and nearby allies regenerate 3% max HP every 2 seconds.
Cost: you are HUNTED. Ambush packs spawn to kill you every 45 seconds.
Term: the Saint demands vigilance. If an ally falls within 12m of you, the oath breaks.

🔥 Oath of Ruin

Benefit: your kills detonate for 90 fire damage in 4.5 meters.
Cost: friendly fire is ON for you. Your attacks wound allies for 50% of base damage.
Term: Ruin must be fed. Land a killing blow at least once every 75 seconds while foes remain, or it feeds on you.

🪙 Oath of the Silent Coin

Benefit: DOUBLE gold and XP from your kills.
Cost: you cannot be healed by others. Their magic dies on your skin.
Term: suffer an ally's healing touch twice and the Coin calls the debt.

⛓️ Oath of the Iron Vigil

Benefit: 30% less damage taken, plus 15 thorns that make melee attackers bleed.
Cost: 25% slower movement, forever this run.
Term: the Vigil never retreats. Dashing while a foe stands within 6m breaks the oath.

⚱️ Oath of the Ashen Martyr

Benefit: +10% damage. When you fall, nearby allies gain +50% damage and +40% speed for 20 seconds.
Cost: your downed timer is halved (15 seconds to death).
Term: a death alone is just a death. If you fall with no ally within 15m, the oath breaks.

Break the term and the oath curses you. A broken oath inflicts a timed curse (reduced damage, increased damage taken, or worse) for the rest of the encounter. The dark powers do not forgive.
Oaths are announced to the whole party. Everyone sees what you swore and what it costs. Coordinate: one player on Crimson Tithe plus a Cleric on Hollow Saint is a classic pairing.

Living Dungeon & Nemesis

◆

The dungeon learns

The Living Dungeon watches how your party fights. If more than 70% of your damage comes from one strategy (all melee, all magic, all ranged), the dungeon adapts: resistances shift, counters spawn, and the run pushes back. One-trick parties get punished. Balanced parties thrive.

Your nemesis remembers

The Nemesis system tracks your run personally. An elite that survives you, or that you barely escape, can return later in the run: renamed, boosted, and hunting you specifically. Debts in the Covenant are personal.

Objectives

Multiple autogenerated objectives per floor, tracked on the HUD.

◆
  • Slay the Wardens — kill the West and East Wing Wardens to shatter the floor's soul-seals
  • Destroy Corrupted Shrines — smash the shrines feeding the dungeon's power
  • Recover the Relic — find and claim the biome's covenant relic
  • Survive the Ambush — hold out against a summoned horde

Completing objectives grants XP and loot and advances the floor. The objective tracker shows live progress for the whole party.

Joining a Run

Four ways into the dungeon. They all coexist.

◆
  • Quick Play — hit PLAY NOW on the main menu and get matched into a party automatically. If the party has empty slots, AI companion bots fill them so the run starts instantly; real players who queue in take over bot slots.
  • Private chamber — create a chamber on the Chamber tab and share its 4-letter code. Only people with the code can join.
  • Invite links — every chamber has a copyable invite link. Anyone who opens it lands on the site with your chamber code pre-filled; they enter a hero name and hit Join.
  • Lobby browser — browse open public games and join the one you like.

Runs support 2 to 8 players. The pause menu notes it honestly: when you pause, the dungeon holds its breath but your allies fight on.

Meta-Progression

Progress that survives the run. Computed server-side, stored on your account.

◆

Every run earns account XP and seals, calculated by the server from your actual performance. No client-sent totals are ever trusted. Spend seals in the Covenant Vault and climb 8 covenant ranks:

Cosmetic Shop

COSMETICS ONLY. Never pay-to-win.

◆

The Covenant Emporium sells appearance-only cosmetics purchased through Stripe. Nothing in the shop affects damage, health, or any gameplay stat, and the shop says so on every screen. The catalog:

  • Hero skins: Obsidian Plate, Sunforged Regalia, Voidborn Shroud
  • Weapon glows: Hellfire Edge, Astral Edge, Necrotic Edge
  • Emotes: Victory Flourish, War Taunt, Shadow Dance
  • Covenant Collector's Bundle: every cosmetic in the shop, one price

Purchases are verified server-side with signed Stripe sessions and webhooks before anything is granted. Receipts are idempotent: you can never be charged twice for the same item.

Minimap & Boss Telegraphs

◆

Press G to toggle the minimap. It uses real fog of war with shared party vision: explored areas stay mapped, and you see what your party sees right now. Enemies, objectives, and boss arenas are marked with shape-coded icons (not color alone, so they stay readable for colorblind players).

Boss signature attacks broadcast ground telegraphs: red zones with exact wind-up timing before the hit lands. Learn each boss's tells and the telegraphs become a dance.

Audio & Settings

◆

All audio is synthesized live with the Web Audio API. No audio files, no downloads.

  • Per-biome ambient beds: each of the four biomes has its own dark-ambient soundscape (instrumental only, never vocals)
  • 22 combat sound recipes: swings, hits, blocks, spell casts, deaths, pickups, level-ups, and boss stingers, with positional volume
  • Controls: M mutes instantly; the Settings panel (CODEX tab, or pause menu) has separate music and SFX sliders

The Settings panel also covers graphics quality (bloom and particle effects auto-degrade if your frame rate drops), the prefers-reduced-motion system setting is respected for screen shake, and every control is keyboard reachable.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

◆

What do I need to play?

A modern desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari) with WebGL and WebSockets enabled. No install. Mobile touch controls are coming soon; for now a keyboard and mouse (or gamepad) is required.

Why does the first join take a few seconds sometimes?

The game server sleeps when nobody is playing to keep costs down, and wakes on the first join. That cold start takes a few seconds, then everything is instant while players are active.

I hear no audio. Is something broken?

Probably not. Browsers block audio before your first click or keypress. Click anywhere on the page once and the soundscape starts. Check the M key and the music/SFX sliders in Settings if it stays silent.

The game stutters on my machine.

Open Settings and lower the graphics quality. Bloom, particles, and shadow detail scale down automatically when the frame rate drops, but the manual quality setting gives you direct control.

Can I play solo?

Yes. Queue into Quick Play and AI companion bots fill your party. Real players who join take over bot slots, so a solo run can organically become a full party.

Is the shop pay-to-win?

No. The shop sells cosmetics only: skins, weapon glows, emotes. Nothing purchasable changes stats, damage, or progression speed.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Note what you were doing, which biome and floor, and what you expected. Report it through the game's community channels with those details.
