HOW TO PLAY

  • WASD / Arrows — move  •  Mouse — aim
  • Click / Tap ⚔️ — basic attack  •  1 / 2 / 3 — abilities
  • Space — jump  •  Shift — dash (invulnerable)
  • Q / E — rotate camera  •  N — next floor
  • H — clean HUD  •  M — mute  •  Esc — close panels
  • 🤖 AI companion bots fill empty party slots automatically.
  • 🔥 Coat the floor in Tar Bombs, then ignite it with fire for massive combo damage.
  • 🎯 Slay the West & East Wing Wardens to shatter Malakor's Soul-Seals.
