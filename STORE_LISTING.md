**DESCEND INTO THE SMOLDERING CITADEL. FORGE YOUR COVENANT. SLAY THE SOUL-FORGE WARDEN.**

**Dungeon of the Covenant** is a real-time multiplayer 3D dark-fantasy Action RPG built from the ground up for instant cross-device play. Whether you are raiding on your phone or commanding from a desktop browser, gather **2 to 8 players in seconds using a simple 4-letter Chamber Code**—with zero downloads, zero friend-code friction, and intelligent AI Raid Companions that automatically fill open party slots.

### ⚔️ SIX DISTINCT COVENANT CLASSES & CROSS-CLASS ELEMENTAL COMBOS
Every hero brings three signature abilities and a specialized combat role—and when your abilities collide, they trigger **cataclysmic Elemental Combo Reactions**:
- **🛡️ Juggernaut (Tank)**: Freeze enemies with your Pyromancer, then slam your Warhammer to trigger **SHATTER (`250% Bonus Damage`)** and pull entire rooms together with *Seismic Vortex*.
- **✨ Radiant Cleric (Healer)**: Brand bosses with *Judgement Brand* so every rapid arrow or dagger strike from your teammates detonates **RADIANT BURSTS** while healing the party with *Luminary Lance*.
- **🗡️ Shadowblade (Rogue)**: Coat the dungeon floor in flammable *Tar Bombs* (`60% Slow`) and teleport behind bleeding foes with *Shadowstep* for a `300%` *Eviscerate* execute.
- **🔥 Pyromancer (Mage)**: Freeze hordes solid with *Frost Nova* and hurl *Fireballs* into Rogue tar slicks to ignite an **INFERNAL CONFLAGRATION** firestorm.
- **🏹 Deadeye (Ranger)**: Unleash 7-arrow *Rapid Volleys*, root elites in *Pinning Traps*, and drop *Smoke Veils* (`100% Evasion`) to pull off clutch teammate revives under boss fire.
- **💀 Dreadweaver (Necromancer)**: Impale corridors with bleeding *Bone Spikes*, siphon boss vitality to heal the party with *Soul Drain*, and detonate fallen enemies with *Corpse Explosion*.

### 🏰 THE 7-ZONE CITADEL & TWIN SANCTUM SOUL-SEALS
Explore a massive, interconnected subterranean cathedral spanning **7 distinct tactical zones**:
1. **Covenant Atrium** & **Pillared Narthex**

5. **The Abyssal Chasm Bridge** & **Antechamber of Chains** (Featuring timed Lava Geysers & Corridor Blood-Spike Traps).
6. **The Soul-Forge Sanctum** — Lair of **Malakor, Soul-Forge Warden**. Slaying both Wing Mini-Bosses shatters Malakor's **Twin Soul-Seals**, stripping his `50%` Damage Ward and exposing him to **`+20% Bonus Vulnerability`**!
